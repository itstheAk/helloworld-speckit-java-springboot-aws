## 4. Gaps and inconsistencies (not fixed here)

1. **Hidden dependency**: email verification needs an email-sending capability, but Assumptions
   say there are no integrations. Not listed in Assumptions (checklist item "dependencies and
   assumptions identified" is now weaker).
2. **Consumer data**: the service now holds email addresses (personal data), contradicting the
   earlier "stores no data" framing and with no retention/deletion rule or privacy requirement.
3. **Weak assessor independence**: SC-009 allows "any reviewer other than the authors", which
   conflicts with the stated intent of an "independent" assessment (lighter than the recommended option).
4. **Self-service vs. goal "most secure"**: open (email-verified) registration in public PROD gives
   limited access control; mitigated by the 50 cap but a malicious party could fill the cap
   (denial of registration). No abuse or de-registration rule exists.
5. **Cap = design limit**: 50 registered consumers and 10 req/s each could total 500 req/s,
   five times the 100 req/s total design (FR-014); the per-consumer and total limits are not
   reconciled.
6. **Carried over**: out-of-order numbering (FR-001a..d, 007a, 012a, 015a, 020a, 026/027), "VPN"
   and "availability zone" wording, stale "dependency" edge case.

Checklist `checklists/requirements.md`: 16/16 before and after by the standard criteria; no marker
changed, but items "Dependencies and assumptions identified" and "No implementation details" are
borderline (gaps 1 and the wording issue above).
